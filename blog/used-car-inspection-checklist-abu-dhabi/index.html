Buying a Used Car in Abu Dhabi: An Inspection Checklist

A used car can look perfect and still hide expensive problems. This is what to look at yourself, and what is better left to a mechanic.

Written by the workshop team · 2026-11-20 · 4 min read

Check yourself first

Walk around the car in daylight: uneven gaps between panels, mismatched paint and overspray can suggest past repairs. Look at tyre wear, check that every window, light and lock works, and make sure the A/C blows properly cold.

Drive it

Listen for noises over bumps, feel for pulling or vibration, test the brakes, and watch the gear changes. Let the engine warm up and watch the temperature gauge.

Paperwork

Check the registration and that the details match the car, ask for service records, and ask about previous owners. Do not rely on the odometer alone.

What a mechanic adds

A computer scan for stored faults, a look underneath on the lift for leaks and damage, the engine and gearbox, brakes, suspension, cooling and A/C. These are the expensive things a seller may not mention.
