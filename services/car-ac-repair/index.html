Car A/C repair

Car A/C repair and gas refill in Musaffah

In Abu Dhabi the A/C is not a luxury. If the air is warm, weak or smells bad, we find why and fix it: gas, compressor, leaks, condenser or controls.

Ask for a quote on WhatsApp+971 50 512 0476

Signs you need this

  • The air is warm or only slightly cool
  • Weak airflow from the vents
  • A musty or sour smell when the A/C starts
  • Unusual noise when the A/C is switched on
  • Cooling gets worse when the car is stuck in traffic
  • Water inside the cabin, or visible oily residue under the bonnet

What we do

  • A/C gas recharge
  • A/C compressor repair
  • A/C leak test
  • Evaporator and condenser cleaning
  • Heating, ventilation and A/C fault diagnosis

  3. You decideYou hear the work and the cost before we start. Nothing is done that you did not agree to.

Common questions

Is a gas refill enough?

Only if the system was slightly low. If it keeps going warm, there is a leak or a failing part, and we find it with a leak test instead of just topping up again.

Why does the A/C smell?

Often a dirty evaporator or cabin filter. Cleaning the evaporator and condenser usually helps.
